# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful and
productive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level,
or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept feedback when something causes harm.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Make room for others to participate.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, and publishing private information without
permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, restrict
participation, or take other reasonable action when this code is violated.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. If a public
thread is involved, do not escalate it there; ask maintainers to review it.
