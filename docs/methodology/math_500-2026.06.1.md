# MATH-500 2026.06.1

Methodology for the `eval_math_500_2026.06.1` benchmark, defined in
[`examples/benchmarks/eval_math_500_2026.06.1.toml`](../../examples/benchmarks/eval_math_500_2026.06.1.toml). The
sampling and seed rules shared by every eval, repeats included, are in
[`benchmarks.md` §2.5](../benchmarks.md#25-eval-accuracy) § Execution, and the
scoring contract is [`scoring-service.md`](../scoring-service.md). mgmt owns the
catalog definition and the proxy/storage path; scoring runs in `pipette-scores`
and generation in `pipette-clients`.

## What it measures

Competition mathematics across the MATH subject taxonomy (algebra, geometry,
number theory, …) at difficulty levels 1–5: the `HuggingFaceH4/MATH-500` test
split (the 500-problem subset, from OpenAI's PRM800K work, of the Hendrycks MATH
benchmark). It is open-ended, not multiple choice: the model produces a final
answer (number or LaTeX) in `\boxed{}`.

The upstreams are MIT-licensed, but (like the other third-party datasets), the
set is not committed to `pipette-scores`/its image; it is materialized from the
pinned upstream revision and mounted at deploy.

## How it runs

500 problems, each completed 5 times and served by the scoring service as
`<id>#0 … <id>#4`: 2500 completions per model, one warehouse row each.

The entry pins `parameter_temperature = 0.6`, the same value
`eval_temperature()` (mgmt's `crates/pipette-plan-types/src/benchmark/mod.rs`)
falls back to for this eval id when an entry pins none. No fixed seed is sent,
so repeated attempts are independent draws.

**Prompt.** Chain-of-thought, with the final answer in `\boxed{}` and the
instruction repeated as a trailing reminder. Free-form generation: no
constrained decoding. The model receives:

> Solve the following math problem step by step. Put your answer inside
> `\boxed{}`.
>
> {problem}
>
> Remember to put your answer inside `\boxed{}`.

## Scoring

Run in `pipette-scores`:

- Reasoning blocks are stripped before scoring.
- The `\boxed{}` answer is extracted (falling back to the last number) and graded
  by symbolic equivalence with the **PRM800K** grader (so `1/2` matches `0.5`),
  falling back to **robust-boxed** numeric normalization + string compare.

## Reported metric

mgmt derives `accuracy = correct / total` over the 2500 attempt rows. Because
each attempt is its own row, this **equals pass@1**: the mean correctness over
the 5 attempts. The scorer also emits `math_500_pass_at_1` (plus `_stderr`,
`_per_sample`, `math_500_unparsed_rate`, and a
`math_500_method/{prm800k,robust_boxed,no_match,failed}` distribution) in the
response `context`; mgmt persists `context` opaquely.
